What Snowball Consult does

Snowball Consult is a boutique consultancy that asks the right questions. It facilitates a shared understanding of the problem, then plans the solution and executes it.

I help B2B software companies pick the right problems: identify the data, the processes, and the digital products that move the needle, then collaboratively custom-build those solutions. That removes revenue roadblocks and lifts the whole organization with better data, tailor-made systems, and better processes.

How the work looks different

There is a lot of chasing the latest and greatest, and I'm certainly always excited about the newest game in town this week. Don't get me wrong: if I had silver bullets, you'd see me shoot silver bullets. The most dependable silver bullet I have seen in three years of this work is the groundswell of raising the floor: data that is clean, thoughtfully implemented with the end user in mind. The next level is bespoke, relevant data. On top of that comes weaving that data into better workflows and processes, for efficiency gains, time savings, pipeline generation, whichever tactics you want to layer on for your GTM. At a certain maturity, everything gets better with better data.

Who I work with

B2B software companies, generally Series A to C, with sound product-market fit and a clear, proven customer base. The leadership team is excited about the possibilities with AI, and aware that wins that move the needle rarely come easily. Someone manages the CRM, part-time or full-time, maybe with an additional head for admin. Sales teams run between five and ten people.

Working together

Snowball Consult works with an extremely small number of clients, one or two at a time. Picking the right problem means that if it has meaningful impact, chances are it is not trivial.

The process is collaborative. I don't hold my cards close to my chest: while we build toward specific data and software goals, learning and educating along the way is a natural part of how I work. You buy the framework for picking the right problem, the planning and execution capability, and a way of working on this type of problem with today's technologies.

I have found curiosity, trust, and a growth mindset to be non-negotiable traits in a Snowball Consult project. That selection has served me well over the last three years, and over my career.
