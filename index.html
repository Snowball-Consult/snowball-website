Point of view

AI on the right side of the equation

Most companies invest AI on the wrong side of the equation: targeting loosely, then using AI to fake personalization at scale. I invest AI on the right side - precision targeting, data accuracy, ICP matching - so your team does genuine human work with a manageable set of genuinely relevant leads.

Powerful models are available to everyone. What constrains AI in GTM is the context underneath it: who your customers are, what a good-fit account looks like, the judgment your best salesperson applies without thinking. I codify that context into your CRM, your enrichment architecture, and your qualification logic. Tools change fast. Codified judgment compounds.

“The scarcest resource in GTM isn’t data. It’s taste.”
What I build

Foundations that make revenue work land

Data enrichment pipelines

Multi-agent waterfall architectures that chain AI agents and data providers in documented sequences. Every value written back to the CRM carries its source, reasoning trace, and confidence level.

Employment verification & champion tracking

Automated detection of who has left, where they went, and whether the new company fits your ICP. Departed champions become warm paths at new accounts, with predecessor and successor links in the CRM.

ICP & persona as executable logic

Hard gates, tiered qualifying criteria, and confidence levels, validated against actual customer data. Definitions structured for both human decision-making and automated scoring.

CRM property architecture

Staging, detection, and action layers with full source attribution. Enrichment never overwrites canonical data, and anyone can read the spec and understand every field.
