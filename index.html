01  Thesis

Anybody can build anything now. Deciding what matters is the most important GTM skill.

“What changes if we build this perfectly?”

The question asked before any build, early and more than once. Let’s say it is built perfectly and you have it on Monday. What changes, who notices, why does it move the needle? If the answer is “nice to have”, it does not get built. Even a simple thing, done well, is more than a prompt away.

01

Decide what matters. Commit.

Judgment. Which data, which process, which product moves revenue in your market, and what is left alone. Written down, agreed, owned by leadership.

02

Build in high fidelity.

Codify the judgment of your best people and run it at machine speed. Every AI result is verified as right, wrong, or unclear. Unclear goes to a human, and the human verdict becomes the record.

03

Educate, enable, execute.

A system that matters changes how people work every day. The team learns what the data means, why one record was corrected and another left alone, and trust holds. Documented, or it did not happen.

Then back to the beginning.

Every piece of this could be built to the highest standard. Most of it should not be.

Knowing which parts have to be exactly right and where 80/20 is enough is the judgment that carries the big goals and gets the small ones done in days. In a small universe of buyers with six-figure deals, 800 good leads against 750 is a business-level difference, so precision is worth paying for exactly there. Everywhere else, the cost of opportunity decides.

03  Working together

Boutique. Direct. No handoffs.

One or two clients at a time.

One person from the first question to the enabled team. Direct communication about everything, including what should not be built.

A paid first phase of two to four weeks.

Discovery with a lot of building. It ends with a written decision on what moves the needle. Everything learned stays with you, in a form a successor can pick up.

Then full steam on what we decided.

Budget three to six months. Upfront payment. No lock‑in. Clients stay because of the value, and can leave any month.

Leadership attention is a precondition.

Work that moves the needle involves people, their calendars, and ground truth only your experts hold. It has to be a top priority, or it will not land.
